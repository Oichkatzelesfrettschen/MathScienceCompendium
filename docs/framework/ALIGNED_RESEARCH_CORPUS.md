# Aligned Research Corpus

This corpus was selected against the actual framework claim clusters rather
than by broad keyword similarity. The acquisition manifest and provenance lock
remain authoritative for URLs, file paths, hashes, byte sizes, and fetch times.

## Admission criteria

A source enters this lane when it satisfies at least one of these roles:

1. Defines a mathematical object used by the framework.
2. Proves a theorem or no-go boundary that constrains a framework claim.
3. Reports a primary experiment for a claimed physical phenomenon.
4. Represents current boundary research that may change a claim's status.
5. Supplies a field-level review needed to find and interpret primary results.

Reviews route research but do not replace primary evidence. Preprints remain
marked as preprints. A recent date does not override a proof, a no-go theorem,
or a failed energy balance.

## Search refresh contract

The current-research subset is a dated boundary, not a permanent claim of
completeness. A refresh should:

1. Query by claim and falsifier, not by framework branding.
2. Search title, abstract, and category fields with explicit date bounds.
3. Prefer primary results and include reviews only for routing.
4. Record arXiv identifiers, versions, journal references, and retraction or
   correction status.
5. Add only sources that change a definition, evidence boundary, mechanism,
   test, or uncertainty.
6. Re-run provenance, MinerU, formula, table, and citation checks.
