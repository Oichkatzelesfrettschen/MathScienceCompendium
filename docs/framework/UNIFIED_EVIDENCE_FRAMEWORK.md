# Unified Evidence-Bounded Mathematical Physics Framework

## Purpose

This document is the canonical integration surface for the retained framework
drafts. It preserves their strongest mathematics, computational work, and
testable questions while preventing repeated prose, undefined analogies, or
borrowed mathematical names from masquerading as physical evidence.

The framework is unified by a common evidence contract, not by declaring that
all included structures describe one force. A bridge between two domains enters
the framework only when it defines the objects on both sides, the map between
them, the assumptions under which the map is valid, and a test that can fail.

## Evidence contract

Every durable claim has:

1. A stable claim identifier.
2. A canonical statement with named objects and units where applicable.
3. Source-document and source-line provenance.
4. A status from `established`, `corrected`, `bounded`, `conjectural`,
   `unsupported`, `falsified`, `repaired`, or `excluded`.
5. Primary or authoritative evidence where available.
6. A falsification test that distinguishes the claim from a weaker control.
7. An integration action explaining where the claim belongs.

Repetition is not corroboration. A digest proves byte identity, not scientific
validity. A successful computation proves only the implemented proposition and
does not automatically validate a physical interpretation.

## Framework architecture

The consolidated framework has five layers.

### Layer 1: Defined mathematical objects

This layer contains theorem-level structures whose definitions do not depend on
the proposed physical interpretations:

- real numbers, complex numbers, quaternions, and octonions;
- higher Cayley-Dickson algebras with an explicit inventory of lost identities;
- Lie algebras and root systems, including G2, F4, E6, E7, and E8;
- affine, hyperbolic, and very-extended Kac-Moody algebras;
- the Albert algebra J3(O) and its automorphism, structure, and conformal groups;
- Spin(8) triality;
- lattices, weight and root quotients, modular forms, and moonshine objects;
- spectral triples and the spectral action when their axioms are stated;
- Hausdorff, box-counting, and generalized dimensions;
- conventional scalar fields, differential geometry, and continuum mechanics.

The Cayley-Dickson boundary is explicit. R, C, H, and O are the real normed
division algebras. Higher doublings are not promoted as division algebras, and
each calculation names the identities it uses. The Albert-algebra ladder uses
F4 for automorphisms, appropriate E6 forms for structure transformations, and
appropriate E7 forms for conformal transformations. The discarded J3(S)
automorphism claim does not enter this layer.

### Layer 2: Typed bridge maps

A proposed cross-domain connection is represented by a tuple

```text
(source object, target object, map, invariants, assumptions, observable)
```

For example, a valid lattice-to-fluid proposal must define:

```text
source object: a named root, weight, or quotient lattice
target object: Fourier modes or fluid state variables
map: an explicit homomorphism or indexed assignment
invariants: quantities preserved under basis and symmetry changes
assumptions: domain, boundary conditions, normalization, and units
observable: a diagnostic with a preregistered control
```

Without all six fields, phrases such as "E8-driven turbulence",
"Monster-stabilized recursion", "imaginary-root evanescence", and
"origami-dimensional folding" remain analogies. They do not enter the physical
model merely because both sides use a lattice, spectrum, symmetry, or recursion.

### Layer 3: Reproducible computation

Computational artifacts enter this layer only after four gates:

1. Serialization and schema validity.
2. Deterministic generation or recorded randomness.
3. Mathematical invariant tests.
4. Numerical refinement, uncertainty, and control comparisons appropriate to
   the claim.

The corrected D2Q9 equilibrium passes its population-sum and conservation
regressions. This establishes a sound solver prerequisite. It does not establish
a beta plane, exceptional-algebra forcing, quantum dynamics, or zonal jets.

The retained high-resolution fluid run is nearly isotropic under the fixed
diagnostics. Its reported peak mode is not evidence for four strong zonal jets.
The current framework therefore treats that run as a reproducible negative or
boundary result.

Fractal estimates remain finite-resolution diagnostics until they recover exact
controls and converge under scale-window and resolution refinement.

### Layer 4: Physical hypotheses

Physical hypotheses must descend from an action, constitutive relation,
Hamiltonian, stochastic process, or other defined dynamical law. Each hypothesis
must specify parameters, units, initial and boundary data, observables, and a
null model.

The current physical register contains the following boundaries:

- Scalar fields are legitimate field-theory objects, but the retained sourced
  wave equation does not yet define a covariant scalar-gravity model.
- Casimir forces and driven dynamical-Casimir photon production are real. They
  do not establish cyclic net work extraction from the vacuum.
- Equilibrium continuous time crystals are excluded under broad no-go
  conditions. Discrete time crystals are driven or open nonequilibrium phases;
  their drive and dissipation remain in the energy balance.
- Gravitoelectromagnetism is a weak-field formulation of general relativity.
  It does not by itself unify gauge interactions with gravity.
- The Planck force `c^4/G` is a scale. Cancellation of `hbar` in a derived-unit
  identity is not independent evidence for macroscopic quantum coherence.
- Conventional tourmaline electrical, thermal, and vibrational properties are
  admissible materials evidence. Novel excess-energy or scalar-ZPE effects are
  untested.

### Layer 5: Excluded or historical ideation

Claims about consciousness resonance, plasmoid consciousness, negative spatial
axes, self-aware recursion, or literal origami folding lack defined observables
or mathematical maps. They are preserved as historical ideation but excluded
from the scientific core. Exclusion does not assert metaphysical falsehood; it
states that the material does not meet the framework's mathematical or empirical
admission contract.

## Reconciled mathematical foundations

### Cayley-Dickson algebras

Let `A_n` be the real Cayley-Dickson algebra of dimension `2^n`. The framework
tracks properties rather than assuming that a property of `A_0` through `A_3`
continues indefinitely.

| Algebra | Dimension | Division | Associative | Alternative | Known boundary |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| R | 1 | yes | yes | yes | ordered field structure is special to R |
| C | 2 | yes | yes | yes | commutative but not ordered as a field |
| H | 4 | yes | yes | yes | noncommutative |
| O | 8 | yes | no | yes | nonassociative |
| Higher doublings | 16 and above | no | no | generally no | zero divisors and further identity loss require explicit checks |

No high-dimensional physical claim follows from the dimension alone. A proposed
application must identify which multiplication law, involution, quadratic form,
subalgebra, or zero-divisor variety is physically represented.

### Albert algebra and exceptional groups

The canonical object is the 27-dimensional exceptional Jordan algebra J3(O),
subject to the chosen base field and real form. The framework distinguishes:

- automorphisms preserving the Jordan product;
- structure transformations preserving the determinant up to scale;
- conformal transformations acting on the associated geometry.

These roles must not be collapsed into the word "symmetry". In particular,
E6 is not substituted for F4 as the automorphism group, and a sedenionic J3(S)
is not used to manufacture an E7 automorphism claim.

### E-series scope

E6, E7, and E8 are finite-dimensional exceptional simple Lie algebras. E9,
E10, and E11 name affine, hyperbolic, and very-extended Kac-Moody structures.
Restricted dictionaries between E10 or E11 and supergravity motivate bounded
research programs. They do not establish a complete theory of observed forces.

### Modular forms and moonshine

The framework retains modular forms, the modular `j` function, vertex operator
algebras, and monstrous moonshine in their standard mathematical senses. A
statement that moonshine "stabilizes" a physical kernel requires an explicit
representation, state space, evolution operator, and stability inequality.
No such bridge is currently admitted.

### Dimension

Every dimension claim names its definition. Hausdorff dimension, box-counting
dimension, spectral dimension, and generalized multifractal dimensions are not
interchangeable. Negative multifractal spectra do not create literal negative
spatial directions.

## Resolved structural conjecture and open hypothesis register

The Fourier quotient conjecture is resolved below. The remaining items stay
open only when they are defined well enough to test without changing subject.

### External Fourier-to-quotient map

The E7 Cartan determinant gives `P/Q` isomorphic to `Z/2`. Every homomorphism
from the square Fourier lattice has the form
`h(kx,ky) = ax*kx + ay*ky mod 2`. Square symmetry leaves the trivial map and
the unique nontrivial map `h(kx,ky) = kx + ky mod 2`. The nontrivial map exists,
but it admits every exact convolution triad: when `k+p+q=0`, additivity gives
`h(k)+h(p)+h(q)=h(0)=0`. The proposed homomorphic triad filter is therefore
falsified. The executable exhaustive audit is
`data/registry/fourier_quotient_audit.json`.

### Matched beta-plane ablation

The historical LBM evolution has no beta term. The new independently specified
barotropic solver includes `beta*d(psi)/dx` and passes the preregistered budget,
seed, grid, and time-step gates in 36 arm runs. Beta changes every matched
trajectory by 0.350 to 0.394 relative vorticity L2. The homomorphic quotient
filter changes no trajectory: its maximum absolute error against the identity
control is exactly zero. A nonhomomorphic filter is a different hypothesis and
requires an independently justified operator.

Primary observables are zonal energy fraction, jet count with an explicit
threshold, enstrophy flux, spectral anisotropy, conservation error, and effect
sizes across multiple seeds.

The short decaying runs do not pass the preregistered persistent-jet admission
gate. They validate the beta implementation and falsify the algebraic filter;
they do not establish a jet-production result.

### Higher Cayley-Dickson observable

A defensible physical hypothesis must select a specific algebraic feature and a
measured consequence. "Higher dimension" is not a mechanism. A minimal example
would compare dynamics with and without a specified zero-divisor subspace while
holding parameter count, energy injection, and numerical conditioning fixed.

### Scalar-gravity model

A scalar-curvature hypothesis requires a covariant action, field equations,
stress-energy tensor, background solution, perturbation variables, stability
criterion, and an observable distinguishable from an ordinary scalar-tensor
model. The generic source equation in the drafts does not satisfy this gate.

### Materials novelty test

Conventional material response remains in the evidence base. A novel tourmaline
claim requires calibrated raw data, complete energy accounting, matched crystals
and inert controls, blinding, uncertainty propagation, and independent
replication. A source paper or registry entry cannot substitute for those data.

### Operational admission packages

The machine-readable packages in
`data/registry/experimental_admission_packages.json` define the present
measurement boundary for scalar gravity, ZPE harvesting, time-crystal energy
amplification, and tourmaline novelty. Each measurable proposal names at least
three observables, three matched controls, a complete energy-closure equation,
all major input/output/storage channels, acceptance criteria, rejection
criteria, and the evidence still missing. Their current outcomes remain
rejected or bounded because none is admission-ready.

Origami fold-merge and consciousness-resonance statements remain excluded.
They receive no placeholder observable or energy budget because the source
language does not define an operational variable. They may re-enter only as new
claims with defined domains, interventions, observables, controls, and null
results.

## Literature integration protocol

Foundational papers define objects and no-go boundaries. Current papers update
the reachable experimental or mathematical boundary. Neither receives authority
solely from recency.

Each acquired PDF passes through:

1. Manifest identity and URL provenance.
2. SHA-256 and byte-size capture.
3. Native text extraction for an independent baseline.
4. MinerU 3.4.4 high-effort hybrid parsing on CUDA with formula, table, and
   image analysis enabled.
5. Validation of section order, equations, tables, captions, and references.
6. Claim-level linkage to this framework.

Tesseract at 400 DPI remains an independent fallback for pages where native text
and MinerU disagree. Longer text is not presumed more accurate.

## Resolution rule

A conjecture is not declared resolved because prose has been harmonized. It is
resolved only when its declared proof, counterexample, computation, or experiment
passes the corresponding gate. Open scientific questions remain visibly open.
Falsified implementations remain in the historical record with their corrected
successors. Unsupported ideas may be reformulated, but changing a claim after a
negative result creates a new claim identifier rather than erasing the failure.

The machine-readable authority for current statuses is
`data/registry/unified_framework_claims.json`.

Revision-specific conflicts and merge decisions are preserved in
`docs/framework/AETHER_REVISION_RECONCILIATION.md` and
`docs/framework/GENESIS_REVISION_RECONCILIATION.md`.
