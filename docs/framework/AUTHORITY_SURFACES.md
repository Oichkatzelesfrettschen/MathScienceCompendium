# Authority Surfaces

The submission paper uses only `papers/main.tex` and its `review_*.tex`
sections. The canonical machine-readable scientific surfaces are:

- `data/registry/unified_framework_claims.json` for claim dispositions;
- `data/registry/claim_gate_registry.json` for executable consistency contracts;
- `data/registry/hypothesis_registry.json` for open and replacement hypotheses;
- `data/registry/independent_reproduction_registry.json` for promotion evidence;
- `data/registry/physical_admission_program.json` for measurable physical claims;
- the preregistration and result registries named by each hypothesis.

Contract consistency is not scientific admission. A positive result enters the
paper only through `scripts/validate_hypothesis_promotions.py` after an
independent clean-environment reproduction is retained.

The `papers/sections/vol*.tex` files and legacy reports remain historical source
material. They are not active manuscript sections. Their open problems and
predictions are explicitly dispositioned in the archival section of the
hypothesis registry.

`docs/framework/AETHER_REVISION_RECONCILIATION.md` is also a historical
reconciliation input. Its quantum-foam, gravitational-engineering,
exotic-energy, plasmoid, biological, and weather-modification headings are not
active claims unless a canonical claim ID and hypothesis record explicitly
admit them.
