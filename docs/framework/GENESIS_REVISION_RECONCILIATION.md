# Genesis Revision Reconciliation

## Scope

Math4 Genesis Framework and Math5 Genesis Framework Unveiled mix standard
mathematics, computational proposals, physical hypotheses, and philosophical
narrative. They are related drafts, but neither is a clean replacement for the
other. The consolidation keeps the strongest independently checkable object
from either document and rejects chronology as a merge rule.

## Source roles

Math4 is the stronger integration source because it contains a denser
mathematical and computational scaffold. Its standard definitions survive only
after correction against primary references and executable invariants. Its
novel bridges enter the hypothesis register rather than the mathematical
foundation.

Math5 is a speculative sequel. It contributes research questions, possible
observables, and narrative context. It does not increase confidence in a claim
that it inherits or restates from Math4.

## Merge procedure

The canonical merge uses the following order:

1. Identify a claim independently of its draft wording.
2. Attach every source occurrence without counting repetition as support.
3. Separate the mathematical object from the proposed physical map.
4. Test standard mathematics against definitions, theorems, and executable
   invariants.
5. Test computational claims against code, retained outputs, refinements, and
   controls.
6. Admit physical claims only with a defined model, units, observables, and a
   falsification design.
7. Preserve narrative claims in history or ideation rather than deleting them
   or promoting them into evidence.

## Conflicts resolved

The merged framework uses the Albert algebra `J3(O)` with F4 automorphisms,
appropriate E6 structure transformations, and appropriate E7 conformal
transformations. It does not use `J3(S)` as an exceptional Jordan foundation.

The merged framework treats E9, E10, and E11 as Kac-Moody extensions with
specific, bounded supergravity dictionaries. It does not treat the sequence as
a measured hierarchy of physical forces.

The merged framework retains the corrected D2Q9 equilibrium as a solver
prerequisite. It records the retained high-resolution run as a negative or
boundary result for strong zonal-jet claims. It does not describe a solver as
quantum, beta-plane, or algebraically filtered unless those mechanisms occur in
the equations and pass matched controls.

The merged framework distinguishes negative values in a multifractal spectrum
from negative spatial dimension. It distinguishes an algebraic identity that
cancels `hbar` from evidence of macroscopic quantum coherence. It distinguishes
Casimir phenomena from a net-work vacuum-energy cycle.

## Result

Math4 supplies the main hypothesis and computation scaffold. Math5 supplies a
bounded question and ideation appendix. The machine-readable claim ledger owns
status, evidence links, falsification tests, and integration actions. Neither
draft remains a standalone authority after consolidation.
