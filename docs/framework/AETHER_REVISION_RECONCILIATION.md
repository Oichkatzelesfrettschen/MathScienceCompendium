# Aether Revision Reconciliation

## Scope

Alpha002.01 and Alpha003.02 are successive editorial revisions, not independent
evidence. Alpha003 enlarges the subject taxonomy, but enlargement does not raise
the confidence of any scientific claim. The unified framework therefore merges
the revisions claim by claim instead of treating the later filename as an
evidentiary supersession rule.

## Revision relationship

Alpha002 organizes the material around a proposed foundation, interactions,
reported results, dynamics, applications, advanced experiments, implications,
and conclusions. Alpha003 expands that outline into scalar fields, vacuum
energy, gravitational waves, time crystals, spacetime engineering, quantum
foam, cosmology, plasmas, black and white holes, wormholes, biophysics,
consciousness, artificial intelligence, weather modification, and roadmaps.

The exact-paragraph audit finds no exact block matches between these revisions.
That result shows editorial rewriting, not independent derivation. Both files
lack a source apparatus adequate to support their strongest physical claims.
Alpha003 also broadens the unsupported surface and repeats roadmap material.

## Canonical merge rules

| Draft concept | Canonical interpretation | Status | Required resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scalar field | A field with a stated manifold, action, units, couplings, and boundary data | admissible object | Derive field equations and a distinct observable before assigning a novel physical role. |
| Scalar lattice | A discretization or coupled oscillator network | conjectural | Define lattice sites, state space, coupling operator, continuum limit, and controls. |
| Casimir effect | A boundary-dependent quantum-field response | established boundary | Do not infer cyclic vacuum-energy extraction without a complete driven-system energy balance. |
| Zero-point amplification | Net extractable energy attributed to vacuum fluctuations | unsupported | Supply calibrated input-output accounting, uncertainty, controls, and independent replication. |
| Time crystal | A driven or open nonequilibrium phase when discrete time-translation symmetry breaks | corrected | State drive, dissipation, subharmonic order parameter, lifetime scaling, and energy balance. |
| Quantum foam | A heuristic label for Planck-scale spacetime fluctuations | bounded research language | Replace the medium analogy with a theory-specific observable and controlled approximation. |
| Gravitational engineering | A proposed metric or curvature response | unsupported | Provide a covariant action, stress-energy source, solution, stability analysis, and feasible observable. |
| Exotic or negative energy | Renormalized stress-energy constrained by quantum energy inequalities | bounded | State sampling conditions, duration, magnitude, and consistency conditions. |
| Plasmoid consciousness | An asserted mental property of a plasma state | excluded | No admission without an operational definition, discriminating observable, and reproducible protocol. |
| Biological resonance | A material or biological response to a specified stimulus | unsupported | Define exposure, dose, endpoint, blinding, controls, uncertainty, and replication. |
| Weather modification | A macroscopic atmospheric response to a specified intervention | excluded | Require a lawful forcing mechanism, preregistered forecast baseline, and controlled attribution design. |

## Conflict resolutions

### Taxonomy does not establish mechanism

Alpha003 supplies more headings and more cross-domain associations. A heading is
an index term, not a dynamical law. The consolidated framework retains useful
index terms but removes unsupported associations from the mathematical and
physical core.

### Reported results are not admitted data

Alpha002 labels some material as experimental or simulated results. A result
enters the evidence layer only when raw or losslessly derived data, method,
controls, units, uncertainty, and a replayable analysis exist. Narrative result
claims remain source statements in the claim ledger until those artifacts are
available.

### Roadmaps are not theory components

Roadmaps describe intended work. They do not define mathematical objects or
provide empirical support. Repeated roadmap sections are retained only as
historical planning context and do not enter the canonical framework.

### Recency does not imply authority

Alpha003 supersedes Alpha002 only where it gives a more precise definition that
survives the evidence contract. It does not supersede an earlier statement by
being longer, broader, or later. A changed claim receives a separate canonical
statement and falsification condition.

## Integrated disposition

The revisions contribute three useful things to the unified framework:

1. A vocabulary of proposed domains that can be searched and clustered.
2. Candidate hypotheses that can be rewritten as explicit models and tests.
3. A record of excluded claims that prevents unsupported language from silently
   returning in later drafts.

They do not contribute verified evidence for vacuum-energy harvesting,
spacetime engineering, biological coherence, consciousness, or weather control.
Those subjects remain unsupported or excluded until they satisfy the same
admission contract as every other physical claim.
