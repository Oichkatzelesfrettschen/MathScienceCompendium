## Practice and readiness

Derive the derivative of x^2 using a nonzero increment h.

<details><summary>Read the worked readiness solution</summary>

Subtract x^2 from (x+h)^2 and divide by h to obtain 2x+h. Let h approach zero to obtain 2x.

</details>
