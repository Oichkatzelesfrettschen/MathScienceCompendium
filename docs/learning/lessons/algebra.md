## Practice and readiness

Let A have rows (0,1),(0,0), and B rows (0,0),(1,0). Compute AB-BA.

<details><summary>Read the worked readiness solution</summary>

AB is diag(1,0), BA is diag(0,1), so the commutator is diag(1,-1). The nonzero result records order dependence.

</details>
