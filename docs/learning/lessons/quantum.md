## Practice and readiness

Normalize (1,i) and state its two computational-basis probabilities.

<details><summary>Read the worked readiness solution</summary>

Divide by sqrt(2). Each squared modulus is 1/2, summing to one. Interpreting those numbers as measurement probabilities uses the Born postulate.

</details>
