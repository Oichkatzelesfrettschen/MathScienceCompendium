## Practice and readiness

Among 10000 people, 100 have a condition. A test detects 90 of them and flags 495 of the rest. What fraction of positive results have the condition?

<details><summary>Read the worked readiness solution</summary>

There are 90+495=585 positives. The posterior is 90/585=2/13. The denominator counts observed positives, rather than the whole population.

</details>
