## Practice and readiness

At Cantor stage n, state the number and length of intervals and the scaling dimension.

<details><summary>Read the worked readiness solution</summary>

There are 2^n intervals of length 3^(-n). The self-similar dimension solves 2*(1/3)^d=1, so d=log(2)/log(3). The chapter explains the additional dimension argument.

</details>
