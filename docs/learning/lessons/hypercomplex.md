## Practice and readiness

Why does one nonzero pair with product zero rule out a division algebra?

<details><summary>Read the worked readiness solution</summary>

In a division algebra, left multiplication by any nonzero element is invertible and hence injective. A nonzero element mapped to zero violates injectivity.

</details>
