## Practice and readiness

Find the conjugate and squared modulus of 3+4i, then its reciprocal.

<details><summary>Read the worked readiness solution</summary>

The conjugate is 3-4i, squared modulus is 25, and reciprocal is (3-4i)/25. Multiplication checks a product of one.
