ABSTRACT/SUMMARY:
================================================================================

Tourmaline is a crystalline boron silicate mineral with special electrical
and optical behaviors. This study evaluated electrical performances of
crystalline tourmaline samples from various deposit sites in China. Results
show that piezoelectric constants depend on crystalline direction, mineral
species, and specialty elements in the crystal. Dravite has higher
piezoelectric constant than elbaite. Dielectric tests show tourmaline is a
low dielectric constant material (4-10 x 10^-12 pf/cm). Dielectric constant
along C axis is higher than other directions. Pyroelectric property is
proportional to dielectric properties and negatively correlated to ferric
oxide ratio at room temperature.

================================================================================
KEY MATHEMATICAL CLAIMS:
================================================================================

1. PIEZOELECTRIC RELATIONSHIP:
   P = d * T
   where:
   - P = electric polarization
   - d = piezoelectric modulus (piezoelectric strain coefficient)
   - T = applied stress
   - Units of d: N^-1 * C (Coulomb/Newton)

2. CRYSTAL STRUCTURE:
   - Trigonal system
   - Symmetry class: L33P
   - Space group: R3m
   - Necessary condition for piezoelectric effect

3. DIELECTRIC CONSTANT RANGE:
   - Low dielectric constant material
   - Range: 4 x 10^-12 pf/cm to 10 x 10^-12 pf/cm
   - Anisotropic: C axis > other directions

4. SAMPLE COMPOSITIONS (weight %):
   HN-1 (Henan - Elbaite):
   - SiO2: 43.64%, Al2O3 (implied), Fe2O3: 0.031%, MnO: 0.43%

   YN-1 (Yunnan - Elbaite):
   - SiO2: 44.16%, Fe2O3: 1.68%, FeO: 1.75%, MgO: 0.029%

   HB-1 (Hebei - Dravite):
   - SiO2: 38.40%, Fe2O3: 6.31%, MgO: 10.08%

5. PIEZOELECTRIC CONSTANT HIERARCHY:
   Dravite (HB-1) > Elbaite (HN-1, YN-1)

================================================================================
KEY PHYSICAL CLAIMS:
================================================================================

1. POLAR STRUCTURE PROPERTIES:
   - Tourmaline has spontaneous polarity (Kubo, 1992)
   - Can attract dusts when heated, discharge powder when cooled (Dutch
     historical observation, Schmidt records)

2. PIEZOELECTRIC EFFECT CONDITIONS:
   - Requires polar crystal structure
   - Even small external pressure produces effect due to low crystalline
     symmetry
   - Non-conductive at room temperature
   - Uneven charge distribution under pressure

3. STRESS RESPONSE:
   - Any stress type produces electric dipole:
     * Tensile stress
     * Compressive stress
     * Shear stress

4. SPECIES-DEPENDENT PROPERTIES:
   - Dravite (Mg-rich): higher piezoelectric constant
   - Elbaite (Li-rich): lower piezoelectric constant
   - Variation results from element type in crystal structure

5. PYROELECTRIC CORRELATIONS:
   - Proportional to dielectric properties at room temperature
   - Negatively correlated to Fe2O3 ratio
   - Temperature-dependent behavior

6. ENVIRONMENTAL APPLICATIONS:
   - Infrared radiation
   - Release of negative ions
   - Adsorption and water activation
   - Important for environmental protection

================================================================================
METHODOLOGY:
================================================================================

1. SAMPLE SELECTION:
   - Three locations in China:
     * Nanyang (Henan province) - HN-1
     * Gaoligong mountain (Yunnan province) - YN-1
     * Fuping (Hebei province) - HB-1
   - Pure single crystals selected

2. CHEMICAL ANALYSIS:
   - X-ray fluorescence spectrometry (Philips PW2404)
   - Determined major and trace element compositions

3. CLASSIFICATION:
   - Based on chemical composition
   - HB-1: Dravite (high Mg content)
   - HN-1, YN-1: Elbaite (low Fe, varied MgO)

4. ELECTRICAL PROPERTY MEASUREMENTS:
   - Piezoelectric constant measurements (directional)
   - Dielectric property tests
   - Pyroelectric property evaluation

5. CORRELATION ANALYSIS:
   - Piezoelectric constant vs. species
   - Pyroelectric vs. dielectric properties
   - Pyroelectric vs. Fe2O3 content

================================================================================
RESULTS:
================================================================================

1. CHEMICAL COMPOSITION VARIABILITY:
   - Significant differences between deposit sites
   - HB-1: highest MgO (10.08%), Fe2O3 (6.31%)
   - HN-1: lowest Fe (0.031%), highest F (4.09%)
   - YN-1: intermediate composition

2. PIEZOELECTRIC CONSTANT FINDINGS:
   - Fixed crystalline direction measurement
   - Dravite > Elbaite in piezoelectric response
   - Element type determines variation

3. DIELECTRIC CHARACTERISTICS:
   - Low dielectric constant material
   - Anisotropic behavior (C axis higher)
   - Range: 4-10 x 10^-12 pf/cm

4. PYROELECTRIC BEHAVIOR:
   - Room temperature measurements
   - Positive correlation: dielectric properties
   - Negative correlation: Fe2O3 ratio

5. SPECIES CLASSIFICATION:
   - Successfully categorized samples
   - Dravite vs. Elbaite distinction clear from composition
