ABSTRACT/SUMMARY:
================================================================================

This addendum integrates the SUPERFRAMEWORK with the Tourmaline unified
framework. The SUPERFRAMEWORK synthesizes Zero-Point Energy (ZPE)-modulated
phenomena, thermodynamic interactions, and nonlinear optical effects. Tourmaline
is identified as ideal candidate for SUPERFRAMEWORK-based enhancements due to
its pyroelectric, piezoelectric, anisotropic, and nonlinear optical properties.

Key enhancements: harmonic generation (SHG/THG) via doping and periodic poling,
thermal-optical coupling optimization, and layered structure engineering for
phase-matching efficiency.

================================================================================
KEY MATHEMATICAL CLAIMS:
================================================================================

1. DOPING OPTIMIZATION:
   n_doped = n_base + Delta_n_dopant
   chi^(n)_doped = chi^(n)_base + Delta_chi^(n)_dopant
   Optimal concentrations: 9.59% (SHG), 8.98% (THG)

2. PERIODIC POLING:
   Delta_k_poling = Delta_k - 2*pi/Lambda
   where Lambda = poling period
   Optimal poling period: 1.0 um for both SHG and THG

3. ZPE CONTRIBUTIONS:
   chi^(n)_eff = chi^(n)_base + ZPE * f(phonon frequencies)
   ZPE modulates effective susceptibility via phonon coupling

4. THERMAL-OPTICAL COUPLING:
   n(T) = n_0 + alpha*T
   kappa = (1/3) C_v v_s l
   Temperature-dependent refractive index affects phase-matching

5. LAYERED STRUCTURE OPTIMIZATION:
   Optimal thicknesses:
   - SHG: t_doped = 528 nm, t_undoped = 486 nm
   - THG: t_doped = 652 nm, t_undoped = 368 nm

6. PHASE-MATCHING EFFICIENCY:
   eta proportional to sinc^2(Delta_k_poling * L / 2)
   where L = interaction length

7. HARMONIC INTENSITIES:
   I_2omega proportional to |chi^(2)_eff|^2 * E^2
   I_3omega proportional to |chi^(3)_eff|^2 * E^3

8. THERMAL CONTRIBUTION (ZPE-ENHANCED):
   kappa_ZPE = (1/3) C_v nabla(omega_p) l
   ZPE affects thermal conductivity via phonon gradient

9. LAYERED EFFICIENCY:
   eta_layered = (1/N) sum from i=1 to N of eta_i
   Average over N layers
