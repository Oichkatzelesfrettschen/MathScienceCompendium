ABSTRACT/SUMMARY:
================================================================================

This document refines and expands a unified framework integrating scalar Zero-
Point Energy (ZPE) dynamics, fractional dimensions, and hypercomplex operators.
Drawing on advances in theoretical physics, material science, and nonlinear
optics, it bridges fundamental concepts with practical applications. The
framework develops precise mathematical models unifying scalar-ZPE coherence
stabilization with boundary feedback effects and thermal-nonlinear interactions,
proposes computational simulations and machine learning approaches, and outlines
experimental pathways using Casimir setups, doped materials, and quantum
systems.

Key areas: Scalar-ZPE feedback loops, fractional dimensional mediation of
quantum-classical transitions, hypercomplex operators (quaternions/octonions)
for cross-dimensional coupling.
